    Before you begin

    What you will practice

    By the end of this activity, you will be able to:

    • 1Acknowledge a customer's frustration before moving to solutions.
    • 2Explain what you can do, rather than leading with what you cannot.
    • 3Commit to a clear next step with a time frame, and follow through.

    Well done

    Key takeaways

    Listen first. Let the customer finish, then acknowledge the impact in your own words.
    Own the next step. Give a specific action and time frame, and confirm they are happy with it.
    Offer options. Lead with what is possible. If you need an exception, call it a request, not a promise.
    Follow up. Close the loop so the customer never has to chase you.
